Hello! I am an entrepreneurial fellow in the [Orange Corners Nigeria](https://www.orangecorners.com/country/nigeria/) incubator programme, an initiative of the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Lagos and implemented by [FATE Foundation](https://ocn.fatefoundation.org/). 

I am also an innovation fellow in the [i-FAIR (Innovation Fellowship for Aspiring Inventors and Researchers)](https://www.orangecorners.com/country/nigeria/) programme, a collaborative initiative created by the Embassy of the State of Israel in Nigeria and the Office of the Vice President of Nigeria and implemented by [Innov8 Hub](https://innov8hub.ng/ifair-cohort-4-concludes-with-19-nigerian-startups-showcasing-market-ready-innovations/). 

My entrepreneurial focus is on developing MedTech and HealthTech solutions that address global and local health challenges. 

When I am not immersed in research or developing human-centred products, I enjoy playing drums, singing and listening to music, giving back to my community, exploring the venture capital space, listening to diplomatic speeches, and catching a good game of football.
